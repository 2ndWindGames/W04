## Final prompt

Create a final square mobile game icon for SOBOK, a serene healing block stacking game. Minimal flat matte illustration, warm cream full bleed background. Three squat isometric ceramic-like rectangular slabs stacked loosely, dusty lavender bottom, muted sage middle, warm peach upper, and one small butter-yellow slab floating just above and slightly to the right, visibly about to be placed. Clear simple silhouette, generous empty space, entire motif within middle 65 percent. Flat solid color planes with very gentle shading, subtly rounded corners, tiny soft contact shadow, calm warm evening feeling. Sophisticated restrained pastel palette with enough separation at small icon sizes. No battery, no lightning bolt, no electricity, no neon, no glossy highlights, no text, no border, no rounded outer app frame, no watermark. One finished opaque square PNG icon, not a mockup.
